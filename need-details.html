Educational Books for Students

Help students at a local community school access the books and learning resources they need to continue their education with confidence.

Education Odha, Nashik Students Resource Support
About the Need

What is needed?

Students at a community school in Odha require additional educational resources to support their classroom learning. Many students have limited access to textbooks, notebooks and reference material outside school.

Support Required

What can you provide?

01

Textbooks

School-level textbooks and curriculum-based learning material.

02

Notebooks

New notebooks and basic stationery for regular classroom activities.

03

Reference Books

Story books, reference material and additional educational resources.

Why This Matters

Small resources can create a lasting impact.

Access to learning resources can help students participate more actively in class, practice independently and develop stronger learning habits. Supporting this need means helping students learn with the resources many of us take for granted.

Community Impact

Who will benefit?

50+
Students Students who can benefit from improved access to educational resources.
Community Information

Where is this need?

This requirement was identified during UMEED's community field visit to a Zilla Parishad School in Odha, Nashik. The information helps UMEED understand genuine local requirements before connecting them with supporters.
