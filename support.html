Frequently Asked Questions

Here are a few things you might want to know.

What is Project UMEED?

UMEED is a community support platform concept that aims to connect genuine community needs with people willing to contribute their time, resources, or skills.

How can I volunteer?

Visit the Volunteer page to learn about volunteering and explore the registration options available.

How do I find organizations?

Visit the Community Needs page or use the organization discovery section when it is available on your website.

Can I report a technical problem?

Yes. Choose Website Problem in the contact form and describe what happened so the project team can review it.
